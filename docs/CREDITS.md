## Dedication plaque

RE-OSCR — Retro Escalation is produced and maintained by **Sarc**
([SarcDetector](https://github.com/SarcDetector) · [@SarcasmDetector on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@sarcasmdetector)):

responsible for the layout you may remember.

- **Legacy appearance** — inherited from the upstream GPLv3 frontend baseline and preserved as a selectable theme.
- **License** — GPL-3.0. The source travels with the ship.

> *“We'll make it pretty later.”* — later, 2015–2026

The Command Console dedication plaque is implemented in
[`re_oscr/dedicationplaque.py`](../re_oscr/dedicationplaque.py). Its
